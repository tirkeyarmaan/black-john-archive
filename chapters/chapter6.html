CHAPTER 06 — "The Crown Was Never Permanent"

Scene 1 — The meeting

The Bulldozer Gang gathers.

The original group has changed.

Ishaan is already unofficially out.

Kajal, who once stood against Divya, has now become Divya's best friend and the newest member of the group.

The group has grown to fifteen members with Kajal's addition.

Johnny stands among them.

Divya and Angel are standing together.

Divya: "I don't think Johnny should be part of this group anymore."

Johnny looks at her.

Johnny: "Excuse me?"

Divya: "You heard me."

Johnny: "That's an interesting proposal."

Angel speaks.

Angel: "I agree with her."

Johnny turns towards her.

Johnny: "You too?"

Angel: "I think it's better this way."

Johnny pauses.

Johnny: "Better for whom?"

Neither answers immediately.

Scene 2 — The history

A series of flashback panels:

Four friends at Sunday school.

Johnny and Ishaan establishing the group.

Angel and Divya standing with them.

More friends joining.

Fourteen members becoming a familiar sight.

The group growing into something much bigger.

Back to the present.

Johnny looks around.

Johnny: "You know I was the one who started this. Everything and every bit of it. Even the fucking name of it! It was based on me!"

Nobody speaks.

Johnny: "You were just some delinquents here and there, and I made a generational event happen."

Absolute Silence.

Johnny: "Four people. That's how it started. Four fucking people."

He looks towards Divya and Angel.

Johnny: "And now you're going to stand here and tell me I don't belong?"

Divya: "This isn't about who started it."

Johnny: "Of course it isn't."

He looks around the room.

Johnny: "You know what? Keep it."

He adjusts his coat.

Johnny: "Enjoy your kingdom."

Johnny walks away.

Final panels

The door closes.

Angel watches him leave.

Divya remains silent.

Nobody follows him.

He helped build the group. He gave it a name. He watched it grow. And now he was no longer welcome.
