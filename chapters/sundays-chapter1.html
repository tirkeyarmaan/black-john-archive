There are certain Sundays you remember for no particular reason. And then there are Sundays that somehow manage to become the beginning of an entire story.

This one started at Mary Help of Christians Church, in Kosabadi.

We had a church event coming up. The Sunday-school students, from Class 1 to Class 12, were involved in conducting the Mass. We had three days to practise, which sounds like plenty of time until you put a bunch of students together and expect everything to go according to plan.

The cultural programmes were scheduled after Mass, so there was quite a bit to prepare. Nothing particularly extraordinary. Just another church event.

At least, that's what I thought.

It was the third day of practice. Everyone was getting ready to leave when Anushka approached me.

I knew her. She lived in the same colony line as me. We had seen each other around plenty of times, but we had never really spoken.

"Hey, Johnny."

"Yeah?"

"There something i would like to tell you...My best friend likes you."

I paused.

There are moments when your brain simply refuses to cooperate. This was one of them.

"Wait. What?"

"She likes you."

I wasn't sure what to say. Was this a joke? Some kind of social experiment? Had I accidentally walked into an episode of a sitcom?

I didn't exactly know how to react.

I think I just reacted with a heart emoji.

Very intellectual response. Truly a masterpiece of communication.

The next day, during Mass, I kept thinking about what Anushka had told me.

Who was this girl?

Was Anushka serious?

And, more importantly, why was I thinking about it so much?

My friends noticed something was off. Naturally, they decided that teasing me was the best possible solution.

I tried to act normal, which probably made me look even less normal.

After Mass, I remembered that Aakriti was in the same class as the girl Anushka had mentioned.

So I asked her.

"Do you know about that girl?"

Aakriti knew.

And apparently, Anushka and Aakriti were going to help us meet.

Well. That escalated quickly.

Eventually, I found out who the girl was.

Aanya Xalxo.

And my first thought was surprisingly simple:

This girl likes me?!

When I approached her, she looked completely flustered. She was blushing and struggling to find the words she wanted to say.

I wasn't exactly doing much better.

There was this strange nervousness in the air. I had no idea what I was supposed to say, and for once, my usual supply of unnecessary commentary had abandoned me.

I eventually suggested something.

"Why don't you write down what you want to say and give it to me later?"

It seemed easier that way.

Besides, I've always found it easier to put certain thoughts on paper than to say them out loud.

A little old-fashioned, perhaps. But then again, not every problem needs to be solved with a WhatsApp message.

Some time later, one of Aanya's friends came over and handed me a letter.

I took it.

I didn't open it immediately. I wanted a little privacy. So I waited until I could read it by myself.

Then I unfolded the paper.

It was short.

It said:

"Maybe I like you, will be my bf"

There were hearts too. Quite a lot of them, actually.

I read it once.

Then again.

I just sat there, looking at the letter.

I had no clever response prepared. No philosophical explanation. Not even a decent joke.

For once, the guy who always had something to say had absolutely nothing to say.

And that was how an ordinary church event turned into something I hadn't expected.
