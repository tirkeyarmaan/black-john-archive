THE LETTER

Somewhere in Korba, Chhattisgarh.

I never thought I'd write something like this in my diary.

Not because my life is particularly boring. Quite the opposite, actually. Strange things happen around me with suspicious regularity.

But this particular incident?

Well, let's just say I wasn't prepared.

It all started with a church event.

The Sunday school students, from Classes 1 to 12, had been given the responsibility of conducting Mass. We had three days to practise because there were cultural programmes scheduled after Mass.

Three days.

Youd think three days would be enough, but coordinating a bunch of children is harder than completing a GTA mission without getting distracted.

The first two days went by normally. Singing, practising, forgetting lines, repeating things and the usual Sunday-school chaos.

Nothing worth writing about.

Until the third day.

THE INTRODUCTION

Practice was almost over when Anushka approached me.

I knew her. She lived in the same colony line as me. We'd seen each other around, but we had never actually had a proper conversation.

So naturally, when she came up to me, I wasn't expecting anything extraordinary.

Anushka: "Johnny, I have something to tell you."

Me: "What happened?"

Anushka: "My best friend likes you."

...

Excuse me?

I think my brain disconnected from the server for a moment.

A girl liked me?

Me?

I looked at Anushka, trying to figure out whether she was joking.

Me: "Seriously?"

She seemed pretty serious.

I didn't know what to say. I could've made a normal response, but apparently my brain had other plans.

Me: "Reacted with heart emoji."

Yes. That was my response.

Some people have romantic charm. Some have confidence. And then there's me, responding to a confession announcement like a WhatsApp notification.

I went home that day with one question repeatedly playing in my head.

Who was she?

THE INTRODUCTION

The following day, Mass was taking place.

I was physically present, but mentally speaking, I was somewhere between yesterday's conversation and an alternate universe.

I kept thinking about what Anushka had told me.

My friends noticed.

And obviously, they didn't miss the opportunity to tease me.

Friend: "What's going on, Johnny?"

Me: "Nothing."

Friend: "Nothing? You've been acting weird."

Me: "I'm just experiencing a software update."

They laughed.

After Mass, I discovered something interesting.

Aakriti, one of my friends, happened to be in the same class as the girl Anushka had mentioned.

I approached her.

Me: "Oi, do you know about this?"

Aakriti: "Know what?"

Me: "That girl. The one Anushka was talking about."

Aakriti: "Oh, her? Yes, I know."

Well, that confirmed it.

Anushka and Aakriti eventually arranged for us to meet.

And then I saw her.

Aanya Xalxo.

My first thought?

This girl likes me?!

I wasn't expecting that.

She seemed nervous. Actually, nervous would be an understatement. She was struggling to speak properly.

I approached her, and she became even more flustered.

For someone who could usually talk about practically anything, I suddenly found myself in an unfamiliar situation.

And the butterflies?

Oh, they were definitely there.

I had watched enough movies and anime to know that this was supposed to be some sort of romantic moment.

But real life doesn't come with background music.

No cinematic camera movement. No romantic soundtrack.

Just two awkward people standing in front of each other.

She couldn't quite express what she wanted to say.

So I suggested something.

Me: "You can write it down if you want. Give me a letter later."

She agreed.

And that was that.

THE LETTER

Later, one of her friends handed me the letter.

I decided to open it privately.

I unfolded the paper.

And there it was.

Maybe I like you, will be my bf

There were hearts all over it.

I stared at the paper.

Read it again.

And then again.

I swear, even the loading screen of my brain would've been faster than what happened next.

Someone actually liked me.

I had spent so much time watching fictional characters fall in love that I never seriously imagined something like this happening to me.

I mean, I've watched Spider-Man deal with complicated relationships, seen anime protagonists somehow attract half the cast, and watched enough romantic films to understand the general concept.

But this?

This was happening to me.

No script. No director. No second take.

Just me and a letter.

For once, I had absolutely no clever comeback.

And that was how an ordinary church event became the beginning of something I never expected.
