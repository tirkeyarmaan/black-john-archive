✦ LITTLE NOTE ✦

You are browsing a small independent corner of the web Made by Armaan Johnny Tirkey an Absurdist Prodigy, anyways

Johnny is an imaginative, independent-minded individual with a personality that refuses to fit neatly into one category.

A student, aspiring filmmaker, writer, and creative thinker, he finds inspiration in cinema, anime, video games, philosophy, and the strange beauty of everyday life.

He has a particular appreciation for vintage aesthetics, 90s streetwear, gothic influences, and the culture of the early internet. His mind is constantly wandering between absurd comedy and deeper philosophical questions, which reflects in the stories he creates.

He admires characters like John Constantine, Diogenes, and other unconventional thinkers who live by their own rules. He values individuality over validation, creativity over conformity, and freedom over blindly following expectations.

He's someone who can take life seriously without wanting life to take itself too seriously — a little chaotic, a little philosophical, occasionally unpredictable, but always trying to remain unmistakably himself.
